{# The stepper. A step is a link to ?step=…: the body below it (step panel, or the tab row under Curating / Curated) is swapped in place and the URL updated, so the selection survives reloads. hx-sync on the link itself: without it the link inherited the wrapper's `this:replace`, so the stepper's own refresh (every SSE burst and every 5 s while a job runs) aborted the click whenever the step's page took longer than that — a step that would not open while a job ran (test, 2026-10-06). The click queues on #tab-body instead, where it replaces a #job-watch poll in flight and makes the next ones wait (collection.html). `global #tab-body`: htmx looks the target up from the link, and a stepper refresh that lands while the click loads replaces the link — a detached link finds no #tab-body and the answer was dropped unswapped. `global` searches the whole document, so the click lands whatever the stepper did. `hx-push-url="true"`: htmx clears a replaced element's internal data, the "boosted" flag with it, and a boosted link only pushes its URL while that flag is set; without the push, the next #job-watch poll re-fetched the old address and put the previous step back. #}
